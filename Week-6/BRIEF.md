# Week 6 — Data Cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis

## Working arrangement

Work in **pairs of exactly two students**. Each pair must select **one** of the four datasets below and use its corresponding student notebook, available in this repository.

| Student Performance | [Open notebook](mia-pr-week-6-student-performance-student.ipynb) | [Download CSV](../raw/messy_student_performance.csv) |

## The task

Complete the 18 questions in the selected notebook using the tools covered in Weeks 1–5. Inspect and clean the supplied messy data, calculate descriptive statistics, compare groups and interpret your results.

Include the required **histogram, boxplot and scatter plot** in the notebook, with readable titles, axis labels and units. Keep your code, outputs, cleaning decisions and written interpretations together in the notebook. Use the cheatsheet as a reference and explain the choices you make.

Add the **names and student numbers of both members of the pair** near the beginning of the notebook. Both students should contribute to the work and understand the analysis.

## Submission

Although the work is completed in pairs, **submission is individual**: each student must submit the pair's completed notebook through their **own Moodle account**.

Submit **only one Jupyter notebook file (`.ipynb`) per student**. Do not submit a ZIP, the dataset, an exported CSV, separate images or a PDF.

Before submitting:

- Run the notebook in order and resolve execution errors.
- Ensure tables and all three plots are visible in the saved notebook.
- Include your written answers and interpretations, not only code.
- Save the notebook with its outputs and both students' identification.

Any cleaned CSVs or plot images generated during the activity are local working files; they are **not part of the Moodle submission**. There is no need to embed the dataset itself in the notebook.
